Camera recordings

Continuous MP4 recording is split into fixed-duration clips. Clips are saved locally with owner and metadata in SQLite. You can view or delete finalized clips here, including clips with no face or emotion detection. The list refreshes automatically.

In Settings, choose how often a clip starts (for example every 30 minutes) and its length (from 1 second up to 1 hour). A scheduled clip may capture any person or scene visible to the camera, even if no face is recognized. Use only with appropriate consent. Unknown or unverified faces never trigger emotion-based spray.

Each fallback checkbox is saved separately and uses the configured sampling interval and spray duration. When an enabled condition occurs and the timer is due, the selected Citrus or Rosemary fallback may trigger and a notification is created. Fallback is not an emotion prediction; identity-based emotion processing remains blocked until the registered face is verified. Continuous video recording
The camera records continuously and starts a new video file when the selected clip length ends.
Configurable
Choose 30 minutes for 30-minute clips, or 1 hour for hour-long clips. Recording starts immediately after you enable and save it. When a clip ends, the next clip starts automatically without an intentional waiting interval. The camera records the visible scene even if the room is empty or an unrecognized person appears. Only emotion detection and aroma spray require the registered face to be verified. Recording requires your consent, monitoring enabled, the PC awake, and Flask running. Videos are stored locally and can be previewed or permanently deleted from Camera Recordings.
